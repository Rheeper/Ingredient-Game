How to play

Every day there's a new dish. Work out what went into it.

  1. Look at the photo. The cuisine and the number of ingredients are your clues.
  2. Tap an ingredient to guess it. Correct guesses fill in. Wrong guesses cost a heart.
  3. Hearts are limited. You get one heart for every 3 ingredients in the dish, rounded down.
  4. Beware the decoys. The grid holds two and a half times as many options as there are real ingredients.
  5. Salt, pepper and water never count.
